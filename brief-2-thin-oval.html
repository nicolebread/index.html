Must say / features

  • Thin band style of the viral Rinfit silicone ring, about 2mm wide
  • 11x8mm oval-cut CZ stone
  • Patented design, flexible and stretchy
  • Comfortable (creators call it "10/10 comfort") and water resistant
  • A wedding or engagement ring replacement for work, gym, pregnancy, travel and everyday wear
  • Great for summer swelling, vacation, beach and pool days

Say it on camera: "the new thin band" and "11x8 oval".

Do's

  • Lead with "new thin band" and show it next to the thicker style if you have one.
  • Show a tight macro shot of the oval stone and the slim band.
  • Talk straight to camera, with natural window light or outdoors (pool, beach, vacation).
  • Say the benefits plainly: comfortable, stretchy, water resistant, patented.
  • Show real use: gym, a swollen-finger summer day, packing for a trip.
  • Reply to comments about band width or sizing on camera.

Don'ts

  • Don't use viral audio instead of talking. Some high-view videos made very few sales.
  • Don't just say "so pretty" with no benefit or style name.
  • Don't mix up the styles: say "thin oval", not "emerald".
  • Don't skip the use case.
