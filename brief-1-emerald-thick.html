Do's

  • Open with a problem (gym, travel, swelling) with the ring already on your hand.
  • Show a tight macro shot of the emerald stone, turning it to catch the light.
  • Talk straight to camera in good natural light.
  • Show the colors you have, or ask viewers which color they want.
  • Say "patented", "stretchy" and "true to size".
  • Show real use: lifting, packing for a trip, daily wear.
  • Reply to a comment on camera (these converted well).

Don'ts

  • Don't use viral audio or song lyrics instead of talking. High views, very few orders.
  • Don't open with a mystery shot or text only, without naming the ring or its benefit.
  • Don't skip the stone name or the use case.
  • Don't use year-specific lines.
